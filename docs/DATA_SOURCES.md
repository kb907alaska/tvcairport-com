# TVC Public Data Sources & Non-Fabrication Rule

**Rule Commitment**: All operational data (flights, parking rates, TSA wait times, meeting agendas, solicitation dates) reflect verified public disclosures from Cherry Capital Airport or are explicitly labeled with `PROTOTYPE PREVIEW` badges.

---

## 1. Flight Data Feeds
- **Carriers**: Delta Air Lines (DL), American Airlines (AA), United Airlines (UA), Allegiant Air (G4), Sun Country Airlines (SY).
- **Destinations**: Year-round nonstops to Chicago (ORD), Detroit (DTW), Minneapolis (MSP), Atlanta (ATL). Seasonal nonstops to Charlotte (CLT), Dallas (DFW), Denver (DEN), New York (LGA), Washington (DCA), Punta Gorda (PGD), St. Petersburg (PIE), Orlando/Sanford (SFB).
- **Attribution**: "Operational flight status powered by NRAA data interface feeds. Last refreshed: [Timestamp]."

---

## 2. Parking & Ground Transportation Data
- **Short Term Lot**: $2.00 / 30 mins; $22.00 daily max; 1–2 min walk to main terminal doors.
- **Long Term Lot**: $3.00 / hr; $16.00 daily max; 2–4 min walk to main terminal doors.
- **Economy Lot**: $12.00 daily max; 4–5 min walk to main terminal doors.
- **Car Rental Desks**: Avis, Budget, Hertz, Enterprise, National, Alamo located inside main baggage claim area.
- **Rideshare**: Uber & Lyft designated pickup zone located outside door 3.

---

## 3. NRAA Governance & Records
- **Authority**: Northwest Regional Airport Authority (NRAA), created under Michigan Public Act 73.
- **Board Meetings**: Held monthly at 727 Fly Don't Drive, Traverse City, MI.
